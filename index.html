▲ Bull case ▲

Why join Finspire

  • A repository that compounds. Every batch adds what worked for them, so you inherit years of curation on day one.
  • Reps before the real thing. Pitch a stock to a friendly room before you pitch one to a panel.
  • Seniors who've already sat the CFA, built the model and won the pitch, one message away.
  • Proof that you spent your IPM years doing finance, not just reading about it.
▼ Bear case ▼

Going it alone

  • Forty browser tabs, three half-finished courses and no idea which one actually matters.
  • Your first stock pitch happens when it counts, with nobody having seen a draft.
  • You learn what a good DCF looks like from the feedback on your bad one.
  • Nobody to tell you that Excel shortcuts matter more than the fourth finance textbook.

You're building something to last your whole career. So are we.
